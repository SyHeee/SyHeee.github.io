## Research Interests

My current research interests include:

### Representation Learning & Recommendation Systems

Learning generalizable representations from large-scale user interaction and behavioral sequences, with an emphasis on long-term user preferences, transferability, and downstream predictive performance.

### Efficient and Scalable Learning

Exploring model architectures and learning strategies that balance predictive quality with computation, memory, and serving efficiency.

### Sequential and Temporal Modeling

Understanding how ordering, temporal information, and long-range dependencies influence the representations learned by sequence models.

### LLM Agents & Reasoning

Investigating how language models can plan, reason, and interact with dynamic environments, including search-based decision-making and web agents.
